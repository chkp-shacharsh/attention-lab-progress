03 / WHAT GUIDES US

Care is part
of the architecture.

How we build matters as much
as what we build.

01

Privacy, from the start.

The prototype runs locally, with session data in memory by default. This public journal contains curated project updates, with no assessment data or third-party analytics.

02

Show what we know.

Preserve source evidence, make exclusions explicit, and keep missing measurements distinct from zero. Software timing observations are not physical timing measurements.

03

Earn every claim.

Questionnaire and experimental task results stay separate. Engineering progress does not establish clinical validation, diagnosis, or treatment guidance.
